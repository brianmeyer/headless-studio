## Draft 1 — plain description

A Google Sheets and Excel workbook for Etsy sellers to enter sales and fees, log expenses, and see a monthly profit and loss — without QuickBooks.

Four tabs, that is the whole thing: Instructions, Sales & fees, Expenses, Monthly P&L.

## Draft 2 — the specific problem

Etsy fees, payment processing, ads, refunds, and sales tax collected all come off the
same payout, so "how much did I actually make last month" turns into an hour of
squinting at a CSV. One tab per job, one row per order, and a monthly profit and loss
that fills itself in.

## Draft 3 — what it is not

Not accounting software. Not a subscription. Not Notion, not a prompt pack. There is no
Etsy connection: you paste from your own export, which is why it costs what a
spreadsheet costs and not what software costs.

## Draft 4 — question, no pitch

For sellers who do their own books once a month: what actually breaks — pulling the
numbers out of Etsy, or splitting fees from expenses once you have them?
