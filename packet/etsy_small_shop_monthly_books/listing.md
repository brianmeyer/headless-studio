A Google Sheets and Excel workbook for Etsy sellers to enter sales and fees, log expenses, and see a monthly profit and loss — without QuickBooks.

## Title (draft)

Monthly Books for Etsy Sellers — Google Sheets + Excel Profit & Loss Workbook

## Price band (draft)

$15–30. Not $97.

## What you get (draft)

- Four tabs: Instructions, Sales & fees, Expenses, Monthly P&L
- A Sales & fees tab where one row is one order: gross sales, discounts, shipping
  charged, sales tax collected, marketplace fees, payment processing, on-platform
  ads, refunds, and what actually landed in the bank
- An Expenses tab with ten plain categories
- A Monthly P&L tab: type the first day of a month and the totals fill in
- Plain-language instructions written for someone who does not like spreadsheets
- Works in Google Sheets and in Excel. Nothing to install

## Who it is for (draft)

An Etsy or small-shop seller doing their own books once a month who does not want
accounting software.

## What it is not (draft)

- Not accounting software and not a subscription
- Not Notion, not a prompt pack, not an app
- Not tax, legal, or accounting advice, and not designed or reviewed by a CPA
- Not automatic: there is no Etsy connection, so numbers are pasted in from an export
